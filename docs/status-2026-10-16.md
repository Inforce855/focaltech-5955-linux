## USB permission diagnosis — 16 October 2026

The next non-root access inspection located the live reader at `/dev/bus/usb/003/003` (device numbers may change). `stat`/ACL showed `root:root`, `crw-rw-r--` and **no additional ACL entry**. The regular user had `Read: YES` but `Write: NO`. udev properties identified VID:PID `2808:5955` and showed no access-control tags in the filtered output. SELinux was disabled.

**Conclusion:** `fprintd` was permitted by *private PolicyKit* but non-root libusb could not open the device for read/write USB transfers. No fingerprint scan was reached. A narrowly scoped, temporary ACL grant using `sudo setfacl -m u:$USER:rw` on the verified current 2808:5955 device node is the proposed next step; it has **not yet been tested**. Never grant access to all USB devices, use world-writable modes, or run the experimental private PolicyKit authority as root. Following a successful temporary test, investigate an appropriately scoped udev `uaccess` rule for persistence.

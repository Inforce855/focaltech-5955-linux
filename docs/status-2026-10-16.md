## USB permission diagnosis — 16 October 2026

The next non-root access inspection located the live reader at `/dev/bus/usb/003/003` (device numbers may change). `stat`/ACL showed `root:root`, `crw-rw-r--` and **no additional ACL entry**. The regular user had `Read: YES` but `Write: NO`. udev properties identified VID:PID `2808:5955` and showed no access-control tags in the filtered output. SELinux was disabled.

**Conclusion:** `fprintd` was permitted by *private PolicyKit* but non-root libusb could not open the device for read/write USB transfers. No fingerprint scan was reached. A narrowly scoped temporary ACL grant using `sudo setfacl -m u:$(id -un):rw` on the verified current 2808:5955 USB device node was **tested successfully**: the next live attempt reported readable/writable access, `PASS: Claim succeeded`, and completed physical enrolment. This is not a permanent udev rule and may disappear on reconnection. Never grant access to all USB devices, use world-writable modes, or run the experimental private PolicyKit authority as root. Following a successful temporary test, investigate an appropriately scoped udev `uaccess` rule for persistence.
